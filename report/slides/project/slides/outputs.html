Two kinds of 3D

A quick mesh, or a photoreal splat

MESH · PHASES 1–9

Solid 3D model

  • Runs on any computer, no GPU
  • Depth AI + Open3D Poisson surface
  • Good for measuring and orbiting
  • Looks coarse, like a clay model

GAUSSIAN SPLAT · GPU WORKER

Photoreal and walkable

  • Needs an NVIDIA GPU (rented on RunPod)
  • COLMAP + Nerfstudio splatfacto
  • Keeps real light, colour and detail
  • First-person walk, like Captures Studio

Indoor 3D Reconstruction

A Gaussian splat is hundreds of thousands of tiny coloured, semi-transparent blobs in 3D. Together they recreate the room from any angle, which is why it looks like a photo. A mesh is triangles, which is easier to compute but looks blocky.
