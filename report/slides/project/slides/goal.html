The goal

Anyone with a phone can turn a room into a 3D space people can walk through.

INPUT

One phone video

1–2 minutes, filmed horizontally, walking around the room.

PROCESS

AI reconstruction

Camera positions, depth and a 3D model, computed automatically.

OUTPUT

Walk it in a browser

Photoreal, shareable, works on laptop and phone.

Indoor 3D Reconstruction

Same idea as products like Captures Studio or Matterport, but from a normal phone video and built end to end in this project. No special camera or LiDAR is needed.
